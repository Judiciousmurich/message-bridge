Message Bridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

App: Message Bridge (com.actserv.message_bridge)

This policy describes how Message Bridge handles information on your device. Message Bridge helps you open text content in WhatsApp Messenger. The app does not run its own chat service and does not use Twilio or similar backends to send your messages.

Summary

  • On Android and iOS, you copy or type text in the app. The app does not read your SMS/MMS inbox and does not request SMS read permission on Android.
  • Message content is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate servers that receive your SMS or WhatsApp chats.
  • Your saved WhatsApp number is stored only on the device.
  • The app does not show third-party advertising and does not use an advertising ID for ads in this app.

Information the app uses

Android and iOS

Data Purpose Leaves the device?
Text you paste or type Prefill a draft to open in WhatsApp Only when you send it in WhatsApp or use copy
WhatsApp number you enter Open the correct WhatsApp chat Only when passed to WhatsApp by your action
App preferences (e.g. saved number) Remember your settings on this phone No

The app does not access SMS/MMS stored on the phone.

WhatsApp

When you continue to WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp Messenger with text you entered. WhatsApp is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect SMS content on our servers.
  • We do not log message bodies in analytics tied to this policy.
  • We do not require a Message Bridge account or sign-in.

Third-party services

The app may open WhatsApp on your device. We do not control WhatsApp’s handling of data. This app does not embed third-party advertising SDKs.

Children

Message Bridge is not directed at children under 13. It is intended for adults who choose to move their own messages to WhatsApp.

Security

Data processed by the app stays on your device except when you explicitly send or copy it elsewhere. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

Your choices

  • Clear app data or uninstall.
  • Change or remove your saved WhatsApp number in the app.

For step-by-step deletion instructions, see Data deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email address on this app’s Google Play store listing.
