# Message Bridge Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 20 November 2026

**App:** Message Bridge (`com.actserv.message_bridge`)

This policy describes how Message Bridge handles information on your device.
Message Bridge helps you open text content in WhatsApp Messenger. The app does
not run its own chat service and does not use Twilio or similar backends to
send your messages.

## Summary

- On **Android**, with your permission, the app reads SMS/MMS stored on the
  phone to show you a list and to prepare text when you open WhatsApp.
- Message content is **not uploaded** to our servers. We do **not** operate
  servers that receive your SMS or WhatsApp chats.
- Your saved WhatsApp number is stored **only on the device**.
- The app does **not** show third-party advertising and does **not** use an
  advertising ID for ads in this app.

## Information the app uses

### Android

| Data | Purpose | Leaves the device? |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| SMS/MMS (inbox and sent, up to 200 newest) | Show a list; open or copy text into WhatsApp | Only when **you** send it in WhatsApp or use copy |
| WhatsApp number you enter | Open the correct WhatsApp chat | Only when passed to WhatsApp by your action |
| App preferences (e.g. saved number) | Remember your settings on this phone | No |

SMS access uses the Android `READ_SMS` permission. You can deny or revoke it in
system settings; the list will not work without it.

### iOS

Apple does not allow this app to read the system Messages inbox. You type or
paste text yourself. That text stays in the app until you open WhatsApp.

### WhatsApp

When you tap a message or use batch actions, the app opens WhatsApp Messenger
with text you chose. WhatsApp is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not collect SMS content on our servers.
- We do not log message bodies in analytics tied to this policy document.
- We do not require a Message Bridge account or sign-in.

## Third-party services

The app may open **WhatsApp** on your device. We do not control WhatsApp’s
handling of data. Use WhatsApp according to Meta’s terms and privacy policy.

This app does not embed third-party advertising SDKs.

## Children

Message Bridge is not directed at children under 13. It is intended for adults
who choose to move their own messages to WhatsApp.

## Security

Data processed by the app stays on your device except when you explicitly send
or copy it elsewhere. Protect your phone with a screen lock and careful SMS
permissions.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke SMS permission (Android).
- Clear app data or uninstall.
- Change or remove your saved WhatsApp number in the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. For
material changes, we will update this page before relying on new practices.
