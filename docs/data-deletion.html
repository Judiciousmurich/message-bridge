Requesting deletion of your data

App: Message Bridge (com.actserv.message_bridge)

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Message Bridge does not create an account and does not upload SMS content or your messages to our servers. Data the app uses stays on your device unless you send it elsewhere (for example, in WhatsApp).

What you can delete on your device

  • SMS list in the app — revoke SMS permission in Android settings, or uninstall the app. The app does not store a separate copy of SMS beyond what Android shows while the app is in use.
  • Saved WhatsApp number — remove or change it in the app, or clear app storage (see below).
  • All app data — Android Settings → Apps → Message Bridge → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data.
  • Complete removal — uninstall Message Bridge from your device.

No server-side data

We do not hold Message Bridge user data on our servers. There is no remote account or profile to delete. If you contact us by email, you may ask us to delete any support correspondence we still hold.

Contact

For questions about data in this app, use the support email on the app’s Google Play store listing. See also the Privacy Policy.
